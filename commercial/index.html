Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and an undersized or failing system reveals itself as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills pour huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff during the rush. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safety

Professional Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched quickly, anywhere in Plano and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems. The priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we set up maintenance and monitoring so it doesn't happen again, and each response is fully documented for your records.
